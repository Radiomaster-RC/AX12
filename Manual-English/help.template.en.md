### Template

The Template Management feature allows you to **save an existing model as a template** for future use, enabling you to **quickly create** models with the same functionality.

In the **Model Management** section, select the current model and choose **Save as Template**. The template will then appear under the model’s name in the Template Management page.

Similarly, after activating a new model, click **Create from Template** to select a template from the list and **import** it into the current model.

To delete a template, click the **Delete** button on the right side of the template entry.
